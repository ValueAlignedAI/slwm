# State transfer, camera substitution and planning

The 512-scene supervised confirmation repeated the earlier cue effect with all 20 weights locked. Uniform action choice incurred 9.47 cm mean regret. With the common physical prior, the two hybrid adapters incurred 0.21 and 0.32 cm, the dense adapters 0.17 and 0.56 cm, and the raw recurrent heads 0.06 and 0.07 cm. The endpoint predictor received actual robot state through an explicit prior. This establishes recoverable goal history in both architectures. It leaves a large role for the task-specific physical model. [Independent results](metrics/supervised.json)

## Camera substitution on a second simulator

The prospective comparison used 512 independent articulated-arm starts, nine prospective torques and both goals for each start. All predictors received video and past and prospective actions. They ranked plans against the same true goal. The clean-camera hybrid incurred 9.90 and 9.87 cm regret across seeds, dense 9.82 and 9.70, and the fitted raw-video control 9.79 and 9.72. The hybrid's excess wrong-camera penalty relative to dense was +0.15 cm with a 95% scene-block interval of [−0.10, +0.39] and +0.07 cm with [−0.23, +0.37]. Neither seed establishes replication of the earlier Push-T interaction. All clean-camera rankers are weak on this task, so this result has limited power to discriminate architectures. ## A second planning step helps an exact simulator; the learned model loses the gain

The follow-up trained a goal-independent transition network on all 147,456 training endpoints. Its state-transition labels came from the 12,288 branches executed between observations. Small readouts decoded joint angles and velocities from each supervised model's shared state. The final 64 paired starting positions were wholly new to training, selection and the preceding simulator oracle test. The two-step planner could inspect the next exogenous action proposal bank throughout.

With raw proprioception and an explicitly remembered goal, learned one- and two-step planning succeeded on 13.3% and 12.5% of the 128 episodes. With the hybrid latent and carried goal, two-step success was 0.0% and 0.8%. With the dense latent and carried goal it was 0.0% in both seeds. The earlier 44.5–48.4% exact-physics result did not survive the learned transition. One-step held transition RMSE in each endpoint coordinate was about 3 cm, comparable to the entire goal radius, before composing the second step. The shared-state readout introduced additional error. A physics control with exact dynamics, a learned model with raw state, and a learned model with decoded state are each needed to localize this failure. ## Cross-modal content after matching duration and speaker

Four-way retrieval used alternatives from the same speaker with identical valid duration. Three alternatives had different words. On 1,009 audiobook windows the hybrid acoustic-unit distributions matched audio to its own transcript in 28.9% and 29.9% of trials. The dense scores were 26.8% and 25.7%, with chance at 25%. For video to text on 72 eligible acted-speech clips, hybrid scores were 26.4% and 22.2%, and dense scores were 26.4% and 23.6%. A further test substituted a different recording of the same words. Only 29 acted-speech cases met all controls, limiting that comparison. Clock-controlled timing and actual word recovery are separate tests. [Matched lexical retrieval](metrics/retrieval.json)
