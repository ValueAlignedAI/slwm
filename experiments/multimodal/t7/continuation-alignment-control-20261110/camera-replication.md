# Prospective camera substitution in Reacher

Freeze both G6 116.50M backbones for both seeds. Supply a 32-tick prefix of camera frames and the past control signals. Fit the same small action-conditioned head on the observed endpoint of all nine torque proposals at each of four consecutive decisions. Withhold joint angles, fingertip coordinates and the goal from the predictor. Compare a fixed raw-pixel projection followed by the same trained head. There is no Reacher physical prior in this experiment. The goal enters only when ranking predicted endpoints.

Lock 1,024 head updates for all six fits before scoring the 512 new Reacher scenes. Use decisions two through four, after a complete eight-tick delay exists. Fix one independent single-cycle donor permutation. Keep the true goals, past actions, candidate bank and realized endpoints constant between correct, eight-tick delayed, wrong-scene and erased camera conditions. Bootstrap whole scenes in blocks of eight along the donor cycle. The replication contrast is the hybrid wrong-minus-correct regret penalty less the dense penalty, in centimetres. Report the separate clean-camera and wrong-camera comparisons, uniform/passive controls and both seeds. This tests whether the earlier Push-T interaction transfers to another simulator, camera and intervention bank under a new matched readout.
