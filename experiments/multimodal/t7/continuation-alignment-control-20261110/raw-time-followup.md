# Physical timing with clocks held fixed

The first synchrony head learned from a delayed latent sequence. This shifted video position information together with content. Its signed-time stress test showed a large preference for one temporal direction. The following intervention was specified after that finding and before examining new people or rooms.

Move each camera in physical time before adding any position information. Crop the audio and four alternative camera inputs to a common valid interior. Supply the identical zero-based clock, length, source tags and validity mask for correct, 341 ms older, 341 ms newer and same-person or same-room wrong footage. A no-sensor input must yield identical alternatives. Train matched timing heads on the same frozen backbones, rows and number of updates, varying whether the negative timing examples contain one sign or both. Match the number of negatives per update. Use 1,536 updates with 32 speech examples and 32 event examples each. Score every head on both signs, wrong inputs, four-way choice and a raw spectral-flux/pixel-motion control. Lock both head variants for both model seeds before test scoring.

Use only G7 training actors and Finnish training rooms for fitting. The confirmation set admits the remaining CREMA-D actors outside all three prior G7 splits and synchronized recordings from the unused official STARSS23 dev-train-sony partition. Verify identities and room disjointness. Give uncertainty over actors and provide the actual count of new rooms. The held Tokyo sample may establish an effect within these particular rooms. Dates, recording sites and trained model seeds remain separate replication levels.
