On real recordings, dense attention gave the higher contact-timing score at both seeds. The study's joint architecture-win criterion is still unmet. These are development results. ## Training exposure

The data pool contains 323,010 base windows or examples, 10,297 additional real impact windows from 832 training recordings, and 4,096 simulated histories with three branches each. The 323,010 base examples include 131,072 text windows, 118,272 COCO images, 50,193 LibriSpeech windows, 12,704 GRID utterances, 7,305 DROID windows, 2,024 AMI windows and 1,440 Clotho recordings. The LibriSpeech 360-hour download is outside these counts. COCO provides still images. Each training batch contains all nine families. [Sampler and registered mixture](../../../../configs/t5/joint_world.json)

Each medium model received 524,288 sampled examples, or 33,554,432 clock positions. Sampling allocated 21.875% each to real impacts and simulated physics, 9.375% each to GRID, LibriSpeech, COCO, DROID and text-only windows, 6.25% to Clotho and 3.125% to AMI. Audio, video, numeric state, and text can co-occur in one example. The family percentages therefore do not represent mutually exclusive shares of measured sensor channels. The byte-text stream uses a causal next-byte loss. [Batch implementation](../../../../training/g5_batches.py)

All decoders use the same learned state. The two explicitly calibrated physical priors bypass it, so beating those priors matters. G5 evaluates a domain-specific control improvement and real cross-modal timing. A claim of general modality-independent knowledge still requires transfer to unrelated domains, correct swap and missing-input controls, and decoded semantic quality.

Six development artifacts each contain 1,024 ticks (43.69 seconds). They exercise video-to-audio, audio-to-video and text-to-both for both medium architectures at seed 511. Each layer carries its state across sixteen consecutive windows. Generated frames are 16×16 grayscale. The generated waveform uses a fixed Vocos decoder. A change under the written prompts measures sensitivity to text; the prompts have no ground-truth movie or sound. ## Where the current edge ends

Keeping the physical state and removing the camera improved the medium hybrid's error from 9.83 to 9.64 pixels at seed 511 and from 10.07 to 9.55 at seed 512. Dense attention also did better with state alone. Camera-only error was about 26 pixels for all four models. In that condition the numerical physical prior is unavailable. The fair state-versus-state-plus-video comparison keeps the prior available in both conditions. These data support a state-and-action forecasting advantage and expose a fusion weakness. [Condition controls](metrics/medium-residual-dev.json)

In the first live decision probe each of 32 new scenes ran for 24 decisions, 38.4 seconds of simulated time. The fitted prior finished 112.1 pixels from the requested goal on average, dense GPT-2 finished 143.8 pixels away and the hybrid finished 146.8 pixels away. Neither learned controller finished within the 30-pixel radius on these scenes. The planner chooses and executes from ten fixed candidate motions after each observation. This is a more demanding distribution than the single 16-action forecast used in training. [Per-scene distances and controls](metrics/planning-dev.json)

The next useful comparison trains on consecutive decision windows and scores the policy on the states it visits. Compare a carried shared state against a fresh-window policy at matched exposure, and test whether a camera reliability estimate improves over numeric-only control when the camera is occluded or delayed. Evaluate all three with the same action proposals and goals. Action-conditioned latent post-training has precedent in V-JEPA 2. Our hypothesis is specifically that multi-decision supervision can repair the observed forecast-to-control gap. [V-JEPA 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985)

For speech, an independently fitted discrete content target would distinguish recoverable phonetic content from low acoustic error. In a matched trial both cores should receive identical training-only units, masked audio and video, and a transcript-conditioned decoder. Word error and same-speaker wrong-utterance controls would decide whether to keep the change. AV-HuBERT supplies a precedent for masked prediction of audiovisual speech units, and 4M supplies a precedent for training on multiple masked modalities. [AV-HuBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02184) · [4M](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06647)

On the fixed 43.69-second impact preview, generated versus recorded sound had a log-energy correlation of 0.049. Generated versus recorded frame-to-frame motion had a correlation of 0.001. Both fall below the 95th percentile of circular time shifts within that recording. The outputs render continuously and react to conditioning, but this sample gives little evidence that they reconstruct the observed event sequence.
