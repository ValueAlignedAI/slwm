**Recommendation.** Write a focused empirical preprint or workshop paper now. A competitive main-track case depends on a prospective replication of the camera-substitution result beyond Push-T. The original two-endpoint test of architectural superiority was unmet. Report that outcome prominently alongside the exploratory finding. ## A result worth building a paper around

A planner chooses one of three 16-action sequences from each starting scene. Regret is the extra final distance to a prescribed goal, in pixels, relative to the best of those three executed outcomes. The simulator replayed the same history before each branch. In the visual intervention the candidate plans, goal, model weights and true outcomes stay fixed. The model receives visual history and the prospective controls. Its numerical observation is withheld and the fitted numerical action prior is disabled. Correct video favored the hybrid on action-choice regret in every fitted pair. The wrong-scene substitution reversed the point ordering in all four. The individual wrong-scene comparison at 202.35M seed 512 is uncertain. The change in the architecture gap is positive in all four intervals. At 202.35M, even under wrong-scene video, the hybrid retained smaller endpoint error than dense at both seeds. The superior endpoint predictor could therefore choose the worse action. A four-tick delay retained the hybrid's lower mean regret at every size and seed, which limits a broader claim about sensor corruption. **Inference scope.** The source interventions were specified before evaluating the 512 fresh scenes. The architecture-by-substitution interaction was selected after inspecting those outcomes. Each size and seed uses the same scene set, three candidate plans, independently sampled goals, and one fixed donor permutation. The table uses a paired circular block bootstrap along the donor cycle, retaining local dependence from reusing a scene as another scene's foil. The 10,000-draw intervals use blocks of eight. The sign of the interval survives lengths 1, 16 and 32 and a four-comparison Bonferroni sensitivity check. This quantifies scene-level uncertainty conditional on the trained models and this corruption. Two initializations cannot quantify general variation across training runs. The adjustments do not account for selecting this hypothesis from the broader G6 study. [Reusable analysis](../../../../scripts/train/g6_camera_regret.py)

## Novelty boundary

Visual planning on Push-T has extensive precedent. DINO-WM evaluated a predictor trained on visual features across six environment suites. DRAMA evaluated a state-space world model for model-based RL. More directly, recent preprints compare candidates from identical physical starts to diagnose prediction-versus-planning failures, and trace sensing disturbances from observations through planner preferences to physical outcomes. The distinctive hypothesis in G6 is the interaction between a parameter-matched temporal-core choice and a plausible visual history associated with the wrong scene. A targeted literature check supports investigating this distinction. Priority remains open. [DINO-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04983) · [DRAMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08893) · [Matched-candidate planning diagnostics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05550) · [Sensing degradation in world-model planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07126)

## The experiment that would change the verdict

Freeze the architecture-by-substitution regret difference as the primary endpoint before any new runs. Replicate on a second manipulation environment, using several new paired training initializations and independently drawn candidate banks and donor assignments. Include incorrect histories matched for initial pose or appearance and physically plausible stale observations. Keep the same information, goal interface, planner budget and candidate actions for each architecture. Evaluate at least one established visual world-model baseline under that interface, documenting external pretraining separately. Report variability across trained pairs as well as across scenes. Test whether the effect persists when choices are executed in closed loop. An ablation that varies the temporal mixer or a learned reliability gate one factor at a time could explain the result.

I would pursue a main-track empirical submission if the interaction reproduces on that second task and predicts executed decision costs under a credible observation fault. A failure to replicate still supports a scoped benchmark report of what happened on Push-T. Publish the generator, locked candidate banks, metric code, individual scene outcomes and accessible checkpoints or a small runnable reproduction, subject to source permissions. The four-command speaker transfer, real-contact overfitting, and speech-generation failure establish useful boundaries for the broader project.
