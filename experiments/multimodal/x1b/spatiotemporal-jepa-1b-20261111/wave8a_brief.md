# Study X2 — Wave 8A Experiment Brief: Joint Symbolic Overwrite & Spatial Control Non-Inferiority Under `Z_mem ⊕ Z_task` Isolation

## 1. Research Question & Falsifiable Hypothesis

**Primary Question:** Can a single streaming multimodal world-model checkpoint simultaneously sustain `>= 95.0%` long-horizon (`T = 1024`) dynamic-visual associative overwrite recall and multi-item ($K \in \{1, 2, 4, 8, 16\}$) selective-overwrite capacity **without degrading** (`<= 2.0 pp` non-inferiority) de-privileged unseen-layout closed-loop control, shortcut-free `T = 160` (`> 2.5x W = 64`) post-perturbation goal-switching, zero-shot sensor-fault detection, or speaker-disjoint spoken physical `xArm6` grounding—and how does its streaming state efficiency compare against contemporary Grouped-Query Sliding-Window Attention (`SlidingWindow-W64-GQA`) and matched diagonal state-space recurrence (`DiagonalSSM`)?

**Root-Cause Diagnosis of Wave 7 Regression:**
In Wave 7, the `453M` `SLWM` spatial checkpoint (`w7_slwm_450m_full`) achieved `57.3% ± 14.2%` closed-loop MPPI success across four unseen obstacle/dynamics layout families (`0.0%` open-loop policy) and `89.1% ± 2.7%` on `T = 160` delayed goal-switching, but dynamic-visual associative overwrite recall at `T = 1024` collapsed from `100.0%` (Wave 6) to `6.3% ± 0.0%` (16-value uniform chance). Mechanistic inspection identified four interacting causes:
1. **Shared Long-Horizon Head Contention:** All `h_long = 9` long-horizon GatedDelta heads (`453M` profile) were simultaneously queried by `SLOT_TEXT` for discrete key-value binding and by embodied/register slots for continuous 2D goal coordinates.
2. **Rank-2 Goal-Latch Gradient Projection onto `SLOT_TEXT`:** `decode_goal_latch_wave7` read `0.40 * z[:, :, SLOT_TEXT, :]` directly through a rank-2 `D -> 2` projection (`head_goal_w`) weighted at `6.0 * goal_loss + 12.0 * rank_margin`, and `unc_loss` penalized `SLOT_TEXT` variance alongside embodied slots.
3. **Full-Vocabulary Softmax Coupling & Scenario Weighting:** `world_model_loss_wave7` shared a single `4096`-way softmax across dense 2D goal-bin tokens (`3584..3839`) and discrete associative value tokens (`64..95`) while weighting `text_nll` at `0.35` on non-missing-modality episodes.
4. **Local-Attention Training Horizon Shortcut (`T = 32`):** At `seq_len = 32`, mid-episode overwrites (`t = 16..23`) and terminal queries (`t = 24..31`) lay `<= 15` ticks apart—inside the `4 x (8 - 1) = 28`-tick receptive field of the four `W = 8` local RoPE attention blocks—allowing training queries to bypass long-horizon recurrent registers.

**Falsifiable Hypothesis (`H_8A`):**
Training at `seq_len = 64` (so training overwrite-to-query lags `>= 33` ticks strictly exceed the `28`-tick 4-layer `W = 8` local attention span and calibrate all `0..63` RoPE lags for `W = 64` GQA) while isolating symbolic memory from task control via the `Z_mem ⊕ Z_task` structural package—(i) partitioning recurrent heads into private symbolic heads (`H_sym = 6`), a symbolic-to-register goal bridge (`H_goal = 3`), and embodied kinematics heads (`H_phys = 9`), (ii) blocking task-head and `unc_loss` gradients onto `Z_top[SLOT_TEXT]` via `fork_isolated_latent_streams` (`d L_task / d Z_top[SLOT_TEXT] = 0`), and (iii) computing disjoint subspace softmaxes over `[64:96]` (`Z_mem`) vs. goal/LM tokens (`Z_task`)—will restore `>= 95.0%` `T = 1024` dynamic-visual overwrite recall in a single checkpoint while remaining within `<= 2.0 pp` of a `Spatial-Control-Only` (`enable_assoc_loss = False`) anchor across all control, safety, and robot-grounding metrics.
