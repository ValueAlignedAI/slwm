## Sustained dynamics

Train from shared per-seed initial matrices, with three initializations (1001–1003) and three 13.25M backbones: retention/attention SLWM, dense causal GPT-2-style attention, and a parameter-matched gated recurrent control. Each uses four 512-wide blocks, GELU and 128 emitted coordinates. All output heads read the emitted field. The primary factorial crosses the three architectures with training on 32-step segments, mixed 32/64/128/256/512-step segments, or full 512-step segments. Each update starts from the exact same sixteen continuous 512-tick trajectories in all cells for a seed. Changing segmentation resets only model memory. Environment state, command history, sampled recordings, slots, masks, targets, learning-rate schedule and 8,192 clock positions per update are matched. The mixed regime visits each length equally. Allow full causal attention inside each training segment. Freeze at updates 2,560 and 5,120, score the terminal weights once on the locked primary cohorts. Report compute time separately from exposure.

Twelve trajectories per update are independent simulated worlds. Four come from the 50 TAU STARSS23 training recordings. The simulator includes distinct board layouts, calibration signs, unequal intervals between new goal instructions, a mixture of rare and repeated behavior taps, and runs with a sign change flagged by a neutral device-change signal. Fresh simulator seeds apply motor stalls. Compare closed-loop time at the requested goal, subgoals reached, holds and probes on 256 missions of 51.2 s. Use full 512-step inference for the primary trained-context comparison. Also test native 64-step attention and resetting both architectures every 32 steps. The second held cohort has two calibration changes. The physical reference receives the same sensors and no hidden actuator sign. On paired no-motion prefixes, reverse both the past tap and hidden signs so all four sensor streams match exactly. Measure the resulting opposite-action decision after 8–480 ticks. Report three training seeds individually and bootstrap entire missions within each seed. The primary architecture claim requires a positive paired interval in all three mixed-context seeds.

## Public real-world grounding

Use the original HoloAssist release with public compressed video, pitch-shifted audio where present in the released container, synchronized inertial signals and time-stamped transcribed speech. Select sessions deterministically by task and source recording before scoring. Use 256 official-training recordings for optimization, 32 separate training recordings for development, 64 official-validation recordings for familiar-task evaluation, and 64 recordings of coffee-machine or computer tasks that never enter optimization or development. Complete spoken utterances become visible only after they end. No narration, future instruction, correctness annotation or future action identity enters the encoder. Keep outcomes as targets. Source groups, usable streams, missing timestamps, time resolution, truncation and exposure counts are reported. Physical interventions are evaluated in simulation. HoloAssist measures prediction from recorded human interaction. All arms have the same beginning weights, added language/motion edges, examples, updates and conditional decoders. The contrastive objective compares the actual 128-coordinate audio-only and video-only fields. Eight candidates come from the same recording. No separate alignment projection. Add a causal signed character n-gram adapter for natural utterances, and an image-difference adapter gated by the visual mask. Rehearse simulated decisions throughout. Use the final 1,536-update checkpoint for every arm. [Audiovisual correspondence](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2017/html/Arandjelovic_Look_Listen_and_ICCV_2017_paper.html)

The primary real endpoint is direct audio-to-vision retrieval among eight nonoverlapping windows of one unfamiliar HoloAssist recording. Evaluate familiar-task, novel-task and STARSS23 Sony panels separately. Leading-source clocks and session IDs cannot solve the task. Include chance (12.5%), raw motion/activity and self-retrieval controls. Report symmetric retrieval, audio/text/physical transfer, masked reconstruction and whether correct evidence beats a within-recording time shift. An alignment improvement must beat reconstruction and wrong-pair controls on at least two training seeds and generalize to a held recording panel. Measure preservation of closed-loop control. A paired occupancy decline exceeding five points is material.

Fit a low-cost, frozen-feature readout for the correctness of the next annotated physical action. End the input prefix before action start.
