# Units of inference and controls

The main context test requires a positive 95% paired interval for SLWM-minus-dense goal occupancy in each of three training seeds. Source alignment is tested separately. In each seed, require a positive recording-bootstrap lower bound for paired-minus-reconstruction, paired-minus-deranged timing and paired-minus-chance on direct audio-to-vision retrieval. A replicated source result requires two of three seeds. Report familiar task recordings, unseen task families and both Sony room panels individually. Never pool mission ticks, candidate windows or action labels as independent recordings. The room-only objective is a separate source-diversity comparison.

For human action errors, fit fixed-penalty logistic heads on frozen fields. Training uses at most 96 action examples per recording, chosen by a deterministic recording hash independent of outcome. Keep all eligible actions in the held sets. Compare all four sources, withholding the spoken words, words alone, raw sensor/word summaries, training prevalence and a training-only task prevalence prior. Use 1,000 recording-level bootstrap replicates for AUROC, average precision and Brier score. Preserve per-event probabilities and recording membership. A pooled 0.5 threshold is unnecessary for ranking. Extracted ground truth begins at least 200 ms after the usable input. Recordings are the independent sampling unit; the public metadata does not establish disjoint people across all roles.
