# Physical measurement and causal cue controls

The two HoloAssist camera streams are in metres in the published pose coordinate system. Microsoft's AHAT exporter encodes radial distance-to-point at 0.001 m per depth unit. We use the original 512×512 PNGs, invert the supplied radial intrinsics, apply the authors' camera-axis transform and move points into common world coordinates. We intersect the timing table with actual distributed PNG names. The timing table alone lists many frames absent from the archive. Calibration used ten common training recordings before held prediction. Initially anchoring every label to gaze at the instruction cutoff retained 9 of 58 sampled events. Moving the *outcome* anchor to the pre-action view and a non-hand surface adjacent to the hand retained 10 of 58 in an intermediate pass. Much of the environment can move between the instruction and the action. The frozen extractor samples every second depth pixel, excludes points within 45 mm of valid hand joints/interpolated bones and prefers a surface 45–135 mm from a valid fingertip. It uses the measured gaze as a tie-breaker or an explicitly tagged fallback. The locator reads the frame before action onset. No post-action frame or target verb is used to select the patch.

Take two frames before and two after the action (requested offsets −450/−120 ms from onset and +100/+420 ms from completion). Use the most recent published depth within 230 ms and distinct frames. Exclude large viewpoint changes (over 350 mm or 45 degrees), invalid poses, unstable cells and insufficient common coverage. In a fixed 20×20 cm, ten-by-ten local grid, require three samples per cell, four-view visibility and pre/pre and post/post agreement within 15 mm. At least 20 cells must pass. Record the fractions moving nearer and farther by more than 25 mm and mean absolute change clipped to 100 mm. A surrounding annulus and the pre-action fluctuation check measure background and registration sensitivity. The main prediction concerns observable workspace surface change. Report hand-adjacent isolated actions separately. Attribution to a device rather than another local object additionally needs convincing blind patch inspection and a foreground signal above nuisance changes. If this fails, retain the proxy scores and withhold a device-effect conclusion.

Optional predictor geometry is computed separately at the spoken-instruction cutoff, including for cases whose future depth fails. The model never receives a future quality flag or the pre-action outcome locator. Train first on the common action targets for 256 updates with geometry unavailable, then for the remaining 768 updates with the measured multitask targets. Keep sample and optimizer schedules identical across instruction treatments. This division lets the first action comparisons proceed during byte-range depth acquisition. Report fixed primary automatic-speech input, secondary acoustic-only and human-transcript substitutions, and a stronger frozen ASR substitution. Closed-vocabulary exact verb–noun predictions count unseen operands as a miss. An additional semantic noun readout ranks the fixed published noun inventory using hashed label features. This exploratory readout never uses held training labels to fit its parameters.
