# Can the model read the cue it is being asked to use?

The primary held-device gates failed, and the source audit found that action words in the reference transcript align more strongly with a short sequence than with the first annotated verb. As an exploratory positive control, freeze each of the eighteen paired terminal fields. Fit fixed-penalty ridge readouts on the 256 training recordings in each fold for (1) explicit action-word groups in the human instruction, (2) the immediate annotated verb and (3) the set of the first three annotated verbs within 12 seconds of the first onset. Use the original spoken audio and automatic transcript, then exchange the instruction at test time. No parameter feedback goes into the primary models. Report macro average precision for labels with at least 20 training positives and five held positives and show the label count. These probes can locate a loss of cue content or a mismatch in temporal granularity. They are exploratory uses of the existing held cohorts.
