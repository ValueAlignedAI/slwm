# Device attribution audit

Before scoring the physical forecast, one analyst inspected 24 hash-selected held events, six per device family and at most one per recording within a family. The images displayed the locator and no speech, verb, effect measurements or model predictions. Twelve centers clearly landed on the device or a component, one on an accessory, eight on a hand/background or outside the view, and three were uncertain. The center audit illustrates why a successful range measurement alone cannot establish a change in the device.

Retain the full registered geometry panel and release its negative controls. Add a secondary, operationally narrower endpoint. Inspect the pre-action source frame and a magnified center for **every** retained held target. Hide instruction, label, predicted scores, later frames and numerical changes. Use D for a clearly recognizable device or device component, H for hand, B for background, A for a separate accessory/tool, O for outside the source view and U for unclear/boundary. Keep only D in the device-centered sensitivity analysis. A single analyst supplies the judgments. Cross-checked device-centered visible surface changes are still limited to the external near-field region. They cannot establish an internal state or unobserved mechanical success. Report counts and uncertainty by family. Do not use these held ratings to modify training, the submitted region, or the primary test.
