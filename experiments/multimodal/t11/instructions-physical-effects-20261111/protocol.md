# G11 · from a spoken instruction to the next action and its physical effect

Register against main `90f00f7` before fitting or looking at held predictive scores. The core question is whether the particular instruction adds information beyond the identical pre-action observation. A scene can make both an utterance and an action predictable. Exchanging the instruction tests the predictive contribution of that input in the model. Human actions remain observational.

Use the original public HoloAssist audiovisual recordings and synchronized sensors. Fold A excludes all coffee-machine and computer recordings from optimization. Fold B excludes both printer sizes and DSLR/GoPro camera tasks. Each fold uses 256 group-disjoint training recordings and a separate development group. Reserve 24 additional recordings per held family by deterministic task-balanced hashing, excluding every G10 group. These 96 recordings form the primary test. Reuse the common familiar-device G10 validation recordings as a separate control. Never fit on the primary recordings. The three 13.25M context parents per architecture predate any HoloAssist post-training. Report device families and training seeds individually. Consider prospective instructor instructions and corrections, with usable completed transcripts. Define the prediction time as the first 100 ms clock boundary after the utterance ends. The target is the first new annotated fine action, with a 200 ms guard and an eight-second maximum gap. Exclude simultaneous ambiguous onsets and duplicate targets deterministically. The encoder receives only the preceding 3.2 seconds of visual/physical observations and non-conversational audio. Remove speech from the context acoustic channel and remove historical transcript features in all arms. The separate instruction channel uses the real utterance audio and frozen automatic recognition. Human transcripts are an oracle diagnostic. No future action identity, effect, narration, correctness label or future speech is an input.

Select a different earlier directive from the same recording and instructor, with similar purpose and word length, breaking ties deterministically. Exclude duplicate text or near-identical token sets. Choose without consulting the following action or physical outcome. Reuse the same decoy and pre-action observations across models. A different directive can still be compatible with the action. Report lexical conflict and compare an explicitly conflicting subset. Supply the decoy's own waveform and automatically recognized content, with the same input slot timing. Also test no instruction, acoustic features alone and human transcript. No instruction identity or file name is a model feature.

## Measured effect

Score next verb identification separately from exact verb-and-target-noun identification. A noun absent from the training vocabulary cannot count as an exact prediction. The physical endpoint is a visible local surface change after that action. Use actual near-field depth, the published camera poses, the observed point of regard, and tracked hands. Register the depth observations into one reference camera, remove tracked hand regions, and require jointly visible cells. Compare repeated pre- and post-action observations in a roughly 20 cm workspace patch. Measure the persistent fraction of cells with a depth change exceeding 25 mm and the change magnitude. This captures external geometry. Internal power state, applied force and mechanical success require other sensors. Never substitute head IMU or the annotated verb as the effect label. Raw pose conventions follow the authors' projection code. [HoloAssist projection implementation](https://github.com/taeinkwon/PyHoloAssist/blob/9d0dfe238ff6ac99c510f8ce8a8c0e125317dbae/hand_eye_project.py)

Sample at most eight qualified instruction windows per recording for the more expensive depth acquisition, by hash before measuring change. Record depth support, hand visibility, viewpoint motion, timing coverage and rejection reasons. Freeze numeric extraction details in a measurement card using training recordings and synthetic fixtures before scoring any held model. Include identity and known-displacement fixtures, neutral pre-action and surrounding-region controls, and a small independently reviewed image/depth audit. Distinguish persistent change of the attended surface from segmentation of the device itself. If measurement or coverage fails, report that limitation separately from language performance.

## Models, controls and decision rule

Use matched SLWM, dense causal GPT-2 and gated recurrent G10 variable-context parents for seeds 1001–1003, all with the same 128-coordinate emitted field. Apply the same bounded adaptation and terminal update schedule within each cell. Cross architecture, device fold, seed, and training with matched actual instructions, within-recording deranged instructions, or no instructions. Keep histories, optimizer, targets, sample exposure, parameter count and physical quality masks matched. Share the emitted field between action and effect heads. Use background pre-action examples from the training sessions to stabilize the action distribution. Add raw acoustic/text/observation readouts, last completed action, action-frequency/transition and no-physical-change persistence baselines, and a training physical-change prior. Report human-transcript and automatic-speech performance separately.

Co-primary endpoints are next-verb top-1 accuracy and error on persistent changed surface fraction on fresh devices. Report correct-minus-wrong and correct-minus-absent comparisons at inference, and paired-supervision versus separate deranged/absent-training controls. Use 4,000 paired whole-recording bootstraps, balanced across the two held device families per fold. Report exact verb–noun accuracy, proper classification loss, effect magnitude error, device breakdowns and coverage. A replicated instruction benefit on either endpoint requires positive lower bounds versus both alternative instruction inputs in at least two of three seeds in each held-device fold. A physical utility claim also requires improvement over persistence and the training prior, sufficient coverage (at least 20 recordings and 40 usable effects in each fold), and the independent measurement checks. Action and effect conclusions stand separately.
