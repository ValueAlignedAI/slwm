# Study X3 Pre-Registration Brief: `SLWM-1B-480p` Multimodal Livestream World Model & Any-to-Any Composition

## 1. Scientific Motivation & Falsification Closure from Study X2

Prior 453M-parameter embodied streaming experiments (`Study X2`, `SLWM-X2` isolated hybrid architecture) established that bounded-memory `O(1)` hybrid GatedDelta + short-window (`W = 8`) attention achieves unseen-layout `H = 6` closed-loop planar manipulation (`74.0 ± 1.8%` vs `63.0 ± 1.8%` for `9:1` Grouped-Query Attention), calibrated `PolicyCommitGate` sensor-corruption routing (`85.8 ± 0.4%`), and `T = 1,024` dynamic-visual associative overwrite recall (`70.8 ± 3.6%` vs `8.3 ± 3.6%` for `GQA`). At the same time, `Study X2` surfaced three concrete architectural bottlenecks that Study X3 is explicitly designed to test and resolve at **`1.022B` parameter scale** and **`640 × 480` (`480p`)** visual canvas resolution across **`n = 3` paired seeds per cohort (`18` total runs)**:

1. **Single-Slot Contention & Multi-Hop Local-Attention Horizon Shortcut:** Overloading one token slot with both discrete `[32..95]` associative key-value bindings and natural-language spatial directives caused a `-10.04 pp` (`[-13.23, -7.14]`) color-localization trade-off relative to unisolated trunks and residual passive decay (`70.8%` vs `100%` ideal at `T = 1,024`).
   - **Study X3 Mechanism:** Physically partition discrete associative memory (`SLOT_SYM_MEM = 54`, routed exclusively to head bank $H_{\text{sym}}$ with exact null-tick freeze $\beta_{\text{sym}} = 0, \alpha_{\text{sym}} = 1$ and `stop_gradient` shielding from all task heads) from continuous natural-language / goal / transcript conditioning (`SLOT_GOAL_LANG = 55`), paired with an every-step `T_{\text{aux}} = 80` beyond-window training pass (`lag = 49..79 > 42`-tick 6-layer `W = 8` span and `> 64`-tick `GQA` window, calibrating `K \in \{1, 2, 4, 8, 16\}` selective overwrites and all `0..63` RoPE relative offsets).
2. **Second-Utterance & Trailing-Silence Overwrite in Spoken Grounding:** Under `spoken_audio_only` two-utterance physical `xArm6` evaluation (`t = 0..15` spoken color followed by `t = 16..25` spoken direction and `t = 26..31` trailing silence), active second-utterance speech and trailing silence overwrote early spoken color tokens (`49.8%` color localization vs `58.7%` direction discrimination).
   - **Study X3 Mechanism:** Pair a strictly causal 3-tap depthwise-temporal Conv1D (`0` future lookahead, `+512 B` explicit streaming carry) and learned sigmoid acoustic activity gate $\beta_{\text{aud}} \in [0, 1]$ with disentangled goal sub-banks $H_{\text{goal}} = H_{\text{obj}} \oplus H_{\text{dir}}$: register `slot 56` queries persistent $H_{\text{obj}}$ exclusively for 2-block color localization (`0` trailing-frame acoustic mean dilution), while registers `57..58` query overwritable $H_{\text{dir}}$ exclusively for spatial direction and goal switching.
3. **Out-of-Distribution Contact Mass / Friction Generalization (`dynamics_shift`):** Under genuinely out-of-distribution `2.20×` heavier, low-damping (`0.90` velocity retention, strictly outside the `[0.55, 1.85] × [0.55, 0.86]` training support) contact physics without privileged physics labels, closed-loop success previously degraded (`25.0%` vs `75.0%` oracle).
   - **Study X3 Mechanism:** Add a causal online kinematic system-identification register (`SLOT_SYSID = 59`) fed `1`-step innovation tuples $(u_{t-1}, \Delta\hat{p}^{\text{vis}}_t, \Delta m_t)$ at `Layer 0`, train across continuous randomized in-domain physics (`mass_scale` $\in [0.55, 1.85]$, `damping` $\in [0.55, 0.86]$) with adaptive push-gain modulation in MPPI, and evaluate against both `Layer-0 SysID-Zeroed` and `Layer-0 SysID-Swapped` counterfactual ablations.

## 4. Leak-Free 6-Way Any-to-Any Composition, `T = 1,024` Livestreaming, & Gate 4 Criteria

All inferential claims report two-stage crossed hierarchical `(Seed × World/Scene/Group)` `95%` bootstrap CIs (including joint paired-world Mann-Whitney bootstrap CIs for camera-corruption AUROC), with sealed-`vault` showcase media exported from the deterministic empirical median seed across `P05 / P25 / P50 / P75 / P95` percentiles plus synchronized 6-column cross-model comparisons on locked common `vault` episodes (`Ground Truth | Frame-0 Copy | W64-GQA-1B | DiagSSM-1B | SLWM-1B-240p | SLWM-1B-480p`). External `0–1B` and `1–30B` published world/VLA/omni comparisons are explicitly scoped as architectural and systems capability taxonomies under unmatched upstream pretraining compute:

1. **Six Leak-Free Cross-Modal Composition Directions (Evaluated Across All `391` Held-Out `vault` Clips, Strictly Within-`(Source, Embodiment)` Disjoint-Group Shuffles):**
   - `(1)` `Video / Audio -> Text` (`mm[txt]=0, action=0` -> subword NLL CE, top-1/top-5 accuracy, F1, & within-`(source, embodiment)` disjoint-group shuffle CE lift `> 0`),
   - `(2)` `Text (+ Context Frame 0 + Action) -> 480p Video` (`mm[aud]=0, mm[vis,1:]=0, mm[mov,1:]=0` -> `640×480` PSNR, SSIM, spatial edge RMSE, `480p` over `240p` PSNR gain `>= +0.50 dB`, **fixed-`Frame0`** within-`(source, embodiment)` `(lang, action)`-shuffle PSNR lift `> 0`, & negated-action divergence),
   - `(3)` `Video (+ Action) -> 16 kHz Audio` (`mm[aud]=0, mm[mov]=0, mm[txt]=0` -> Log-Spectral Distance in dB, onset envelope correlation, playable `16 kHz` Griffin-Lim `.wav` synthesis, & within-`(source, embodiment)` disjoint-group shuffle LSD lift `> 0`),
   - `(4)` `Text / Video -> Physical 3D Movement` (`mm[mov]=0, action=0` on true kinematic sources `0..4` -> `1`-step ADE, `4`-step FDE, & within-`(source, embodiment)` disjoint-group shuffle MSE lift `> 0`),
   - `(5)` `Video (+ Text; + Audio on Spoken xArm6) -> Continuous Robot Actions` (`has_true_action=1, action=0, mm[mov,1:]=0` -> `MSE`, `L1`, cosine alignment, & fixed-`t=0` within-`(source, embodiment)` disjoint-group `(Video+Text/AV)` shuffle MSE lift `> 0`),
   - `(6)` `Blind Audio (+ Proprio when present) -> Occluded 480p Visual Motion/Impact & PolicyCommitGate` (`mm[vis]=0, action=0` scored against genuinely occluded `480p` visual frame-difference motion `||V_t - V_{t-1}||_1 > 0.025` -> fixed-proprioception within-`(source, embodiment)` shuffled-audio BCE lift `> 0`, occluded motion AUROC, + unseen 4-corruption `480p` camera OOD AUROC, selective routing `>= 82%`, & 10-bin binary ECE).
2. **Continuous Causal `B = 1` Livestream (`T = 1,024` Ticks @ `480p`):**

3. **Embodied Control & Spoken Grounding Non-Regression:**
