# G9 first matched results

All 22 four-layer fits reached 384 updates. With a wide 512-coordinate GELU field, SLWM spent 7.82% and 7.84% of held time at the requested goal. Dense GPT-2 reached 9.91% and 11.46%. Both paired intervals favored dense GPT-2. The sensor-based controller reached every subgoal and averaged 96.24% occupancy over the same 128 missions.

The first complete large pair (training seed 912, 4,096 updates, 116.70M trainable parameters, 128 emitted coordinates) reversed that outcome. SLWM averaged 94.11% occupancy and reached all eight subgoals in all 128 missions. Dense GPT-2 averaged 31.88% occupancy and reached 67.87% of subgoals. The within-mission paired occupancy difference was +62.23 percentage points, with a 95% interval of +60.56 to +63.92. Resetting SLWM every step reduced occupancy to 18.42%. Reversing the recorded sign of earlier taps reduced it to 14.66%. On sensor-identical histories that differed only in the issued tap, its terminal action matched the expert in 100% of trials. The corresponding dense score was 77.73%.

The architectures' native memory policies differed. Dense attention kept the most recent 64 clock positions. Retention could carry information through the full mission. A full-attention-cache control and matched state resets are registered on a new cohort. The larger run had over ten times the exposure of the small screen. A small-trunk continuation from the same initialization with the large schedule will compare size at equal exposure. [History and exposure controls](history-and-exposure.md)

Near-perfect simulator control did not imply the best real sensor model. In this large 128-coordinate pair, the hybrid had higher masked audio error and higher event-text loss on the held Sony recordings. In the small screen, periodic activations improved wide-field development occupancy for both architectures in seed 911 and for neither in seed 912. They missed the registered promotion rule.

The 4,096-step fresh runs have 393,216 separate simulator episodes and 16,777,216 sampled clock positions. Training also uses 2.87 measured hours from 50 recordings in seven rooms. About half of the windows expose all four streams together. On real recordings, an average tick has 1.03 annotated event words. The separate contact panel contains 48 timestamp-joined trials from 24 robot scenes, split into six calibration and eighteen held scenes.
