# G14 development decisions

The initial acoustic model selected aligned audio at seed 1403. It recovered all three spoken fields on 73.7% of development clips. The best audiovisual fit was 70.8%. These are whole-utterance recognition results; a publicly supplied four-station map can disambiguate some letters and digits. The first reserved-speaker recognition scores were inspected after freezing this edge. Add an externally pretrained Whisper-small.en encoder with a fixed 64-coordinate projection of native recorded audio, then rerun the aligned audio and audiovisual heads at the same three seeds and exposures. Select the deployment edge by the original development NLL rule, calibrate abstention on the existing separate calibration voices, and preserve the original edge as an end-to-end control. The held *physical* outcomes have not been opened.

In a 16-world physical pilot, driving straight through the object achieved 0/32 placements. The approach-and-push controller achieved 24/32 arrivals and 17/32 selective successes. Oracle object sensing reached 30/32 arrivals and 22/32 selective successes. Actual camera calibration on 32 separate starts gave median localization errors between 0.7 and 3.3 mm across the four colors. A geometry-only approach loses success through contact with other blocks. Compare an obstacle-aware waypoint policy with the direct route before choosing a shared routing option. The main first-wave training bank has 320 independent worlds, 4,698 physically branched states, eight candidate controls per state and three native contact steps per candidate. An independent 48-world bank supplies 676 development forks. A development branch is never a held physical rollout.
