# Cross-worker reset audit

The original 32-world development comparison restored a single physical snapshot for every policy and spoken branch within each start. When a later job regenerated an earlier seed on a worker that had already run a different world, only eight of its 32 simulator digests matched the earlier job. Station coordinates matched in all 32. Across the two refresh banks, the largest initial effector difference was 2.33 mm and the largest RGB-derived block estimate difference was 4.32 mm. This made it unsafe to attribute differences between those separately generated jobs entirely to the training distribution.

Retain the original 12 continuation fits and their raw banks as an exploratory reset audit. Recreate both 128-world banks and the 32-world value development bank in fresh Bullet clients. Match every training start in body, joint, friction, motor and camera information. Require equal start digests, RGB bytes, measured effector history, estimated object history and candidate actions at the zero clock. Refit the same 12 recipes and publish both checkpoint lineages. On development compare all 18 original, teacher-refresh and learner-refresh seed/model recipes within one saved state per scene. Check native hashes across the independently regenerated contact-gate study. Create a new client for every primary held world. No held physical outcomes had been read when this correction was registered.
