# Test the complete trajectory, including simulator state invisible to policy and evaluator

Replaying a complete closed-loop policy after restoring a PyBullet snapshot and interleaving other rollouts uncovered a second issue. Eight diagnostic worlds all regained the same measured actuator and body state, yet seven repeated trajectories differed in their coordinates. The largest within-trajectory object difference was 72.24 mm. Identical observed initial values alone were insufficient to establish reproducible physical counterfactuals.

Run each evaluation branch in a fork of the untouched, fully initialized Linux DIRECT process. Each policy inherits an identical copy of all solver memory, including caches. The parent never advances after snapshot creation. Decode and score each child independently. Regenerate the whole within-world comparison of training distributions, field processors and contact gates under this isolation. Require a full 72-step replay with exactly identical poses and camera observations on all eight diagnostic starts before evaluating the held worlds. Regenerate each long-mission policy from a fresh client as well.

Quantify ordinary snapshot sensitivity separately at the shorter horizons used for training. Use 24 fresh development starts, 120 contact and approach states, eight candidate actions, exact cloned-process references and the actual original candidate ordering. Report distributions for the one-macro physical effects, delayed return labels, and lost return from picking the different candidate. Keep the exploratory data and receipts visible.

Use scientific tolerances relative to the observed roughly 2.6 mm one-step prediction error and 12 mm delayed candidate regret. Retain the supervision if its mean one-step effect difference is at most 0.25 mm, the mean absolute delayed return difference is at most 1 mm, and the mean lost return from the changed ranking is at most 0.5 mm. Otherwise, regenerate fork labels with untouched process copies and refit matched continuations before admitting held outcomes.

The original one-step effect discrepancy averaged 0.0482 mm. The delayed return discrepancy averaged 1.20 mm, and choosing the altered ranking cost 1.03 mm on average. Keep the common one-step training bank in every arm and regenerate the teacher, learner and value-development continuation banks with an independent process for each candidate. Refit both architectures at all three seeds. Repeat the audit against the reversed order of isolated candidates. These decisions precede held execution.

Recheck the originally selected direct route on all 32 development starts with independent solver copies for every geometry, dense and spatial branch at seed 1411. This replication is diagnostic. The common route supplied to the training collectors and held controllers stays fixed.
