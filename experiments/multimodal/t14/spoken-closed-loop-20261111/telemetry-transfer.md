# Actuator transfer from simulation

Generate 192 independent native Language-Table starts at 5 Hz, matching the acquisition clock of the real dataset. Use 40 actions per start. Choose short control sequences from the real training split near the current effector position. The simulator supplies the response labels. Fit the same causal linear response equation previously released for physical telemetry. Predict measured effector displacement after one through four specified controls. Score the previously released G13 prospective and readout cohorts separately. Neither cohort participates in fitting, model choice or action-sequence sampling.

Compare no movement, cumulative commanded movement, simulation-only identification, calibration from 1, 4, 16 or 64 real training parent recordings, real-only training with those identical recordings, and all 583 real training parents. Use five nested calibration orders, average errors across training orders, and bootstrap whole real recording parents. This tests the transfer of actuator response. Table contact success in the new randomized-goal experiment remains a separate simulator measurement.
