# Independent units in the spoken-control experiment

The eight-speaker cohort schedules 1,024 human commands using 311 distinct original recordings and 169 quartets. The two new speakers contribute 256 scheduled commands from 80 distinct recordings and 40 quartets. Each of the 320 physical starts is independently seeded. All four sibling goals use the same speaker and physical start. None of the 1,981 evaluation sentences appears in local training, development or calibration. The two new speakers and all held physics seeds are also disjoint from the locally fitted data. The frozen external speech encoder carries its own upstream pretraining.
