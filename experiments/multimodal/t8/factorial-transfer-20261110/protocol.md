# G8 factorization of multimodal transfer

## Question and intervention

Does changing speech, text and real audiovisual examples improve action decisions when physical training trajectories stay fixed? G7 reduced held Push-T error in all eight model, size and seed comparisons, while replacing five families together. Its later camera ablation found little incremental value from video when proprioception was available. [G7 outcome](../../t7/continuation-alignment-control-20261110/report.md) · [Sensor and plan ablations](../../t7/continuation-alignment-control-20261110/metrics/input-controls.json)

Use the 116,502,025-parameter hybrid and all-attention dense GPT-2-style model, two seeds, and the same four G6 step-8,192 optimizer states as G7. The two factors are the G7 replacement of simulated starts and its joint replacement of acted speech, audiobooks, independent documents and synchronized real events. The other four corpus families, pretraining heads, effective batch of 256, per-row source/target/validity masks, random order and learning-rate schedule are held fixed. Each new arm runs 8,192 further updates. The existing all-replay and all-fresh terminals supply two corners. Train the two missing corners for each core and seed, eight new fits in total. The non-simulator intervention bundles four families. A positive effect identifies that bundle; it cannot assign credit to an individual modality.

Before confirming, verify exact input factorization against the archived G7 sampler, and compare four independent 2,048-update numerical bridge runs with G7's original weights at update 10,240. Counterbalance replay and fully fresh bridge arms over architecture and seed. Prefer the same v5e hardware. Record any numerical or backend difference. A bridge mismatch requires a disclosed compatibility analysis and additional matched controls before making a cross-wave causal claim. Primary comparisons use common optimizer exposure. Measure model-update time and full billed runtime separately.

## Prospective tests

1. **Action decisions in familiar physics.** Reserve 2,048 new replay-checked Push-T starts. Each has the same prefix for three executed alternative controls. Sample two independent target displacements and their opposites per scene before scoring. Rank the plans by predicted goal distance and use realized excess distance over the best offered action as the primary outcome. Endpoint error, branch differences, optimal-choice rate, raw geometry, uniform/passive predictions, camera removal, camera substitution and prospective-action erasure are controls.
2. **Zero-shot transfer to new physical dynamics.** Reserve 2,048 scenes of pushing a circular puck beside a stationary barrier using the same state and waypoint interface. The object and collision geometry differ from the T-shaped training object. Record actual barrier contacts and plan separability. Use the same four-goal decision rule and measured branch outcomes. This is transfer within planar pushing, with the input interface deliberately kept compatible.
3. **Transfer across simulator and action interface.** Use frozen states and identical train-only readouts on 512 training Reacher starts and 512 newly reserved MuJoCo starts. The articulated arm uses joint torques. Score goal-conditioned ranking against the fitted raw features, parent controls and matched dense backbones. Report head size, fitting exposure and a physical-prior control. The G7 foundation had no Reacher training. Its previous post-training investigation informed the choice of this task.
4. **Sustained decisions.** Run 24 consecutive receding-horizon decisions on 64 further starts per pushing task, executing 16 physical ticks per decision. Keep goal, initial state and proposal RNG paired across controllers. Supply only the observed 16-tick history and candidate plans at each decision and record terminal goal distance, success within 20 pixels and trajectory duration. Terminal success is a separate measure from single-decision regret.
5. **Language and sensing checks.** Reuse the fixed G7 held independent FineWeb, speech and real-room panels to check retention and whether changing the intended family produces a measurable effect. Label these reused diagnostics. Preserve token, source and target units.

Lock the generator, scoring choices, immutable data hashes, all 16 terminal weight identities and the four zero-additional-update parents before opening G8 confirmation outcomes. Average within physical start, then bootstrap starts with the same 4,000 paired draws. Report each seed. Report simple effects of the non-simulator factor at both levels of simulator freshness, simple simulator effects, the averaged main effects, their interaction, and architecture differences. Negative error or regret changes favor the new data. For the primary claim of positive cross-modal decision transfer, require a reduction in mean joint decision regret attributable to the non-simulator factor on both pushing tasks for both hybrid seeds, with simultaneous one-sided bounds over those four endpoints (Bonferroni familywise 5%). Show the dense comparison even if this criterion fails. A hybrid-specific claim additionally requires a paired architecture interaction. Cross-interface Reacher and long-horizon outcomes are separately reported. Two training seeds limit architecture-level inference.
