# Acoustic bit-allocation follow-up

The predeclared 2,250-bit/s product-code oracle scored 100.1% WER on 132 previously unused test-other clips from 33 speakers. Their original audio scored 25.5% and the fine-mel path scored 25.4%. The train-fitted PCA retained 98.2% of spectral variance. An engineering check on the first 20 clips found much larger distortion after quantization than after continuous PCA projection. These results motivate a new comparison, registered after that observation and before scoring a new panel.

Keep the same train-fitted PCA, fine-frame pairing, training people and frozen Vocos/Whisper chain. Fit scalar Lloyd quantizers on the training component distributions. Compare equal bit allocation across coordinates (higher-variance components receive the remainder) with allocation optimized by dynamic programming for empirical training distortion. Test fixed payloads of 6, 24, 48, 96 and 192 bits per frame pair. The primary paired contrast is optimized minus equal scalar WER at 48 bits, or 2,250 nominal bits per second. Include the original product code at that rate, the continuous PCA reconstruction, unquantized fine mels and original audio. Verify actual packed bits round-trip. Report codebook size and byte padding apart from nominal stream rate.

Hold out four predetermined clips per speaker from the official **dev-other** release. Verify that its people are disjoint from the G7 fitting speakers and from the preceding G8 test-other panel. Use the archive with official MD5 `c8d0bcc9cca99d4f8b62fcc847357931`. For acted speech, use four other recordings per person from the same 14 timing actors. Disclose their repeated identity and exclude every clip in the original G8 waveform panel. The audiobook contrast is primary. [Source documentation and checksums](https://www.openslr.org/12/)

Fit four matched, fixed-budget predictors of the continuous acoustic coefficients from the existing frozen G6 fields, with 2,048 updates each on original G7 training people. Decode their predicted coefficients with the registered allocation. Include correct, wrong and erased sources, and compare copied-context gap repair to a raw interpolation control. Treat acoustic reconstruction with observed target sound separately from predicting an entire waveform from text or video. Freeze the allocation and predictors before fresh recognition. This follow-up uses the Fedora 3090 independently of the factorial training.
