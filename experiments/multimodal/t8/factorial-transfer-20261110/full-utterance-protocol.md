# Independent full-utterance codec replication

After the registered dev-other bit-allocation result, test the released, immutable codebook on a third audiobook population. Use the official LibriSpeech test-clean archive with MD5 `32fa31d27d2e1cad72775fee3f4849a9`. Before recognition select two whole 4–18-second utterances per eligible reader by the fixed hash salt `g8-full-codec-81301/`. Exclude speakers present in either prior G8 audiobook confirmation or G7 training. Use the complete published transcript and waveform. This panel uses no CTC alignment to select word boundaries.

Freeze the existing codebook, its allocations and the renderer. Compare original PCM, fine-mel reconstruction, equal and training-distortion allocations at 2,250 bits/s, and trained allocation at 9,000 bits/s. Score with the original Whisper-tiny.en and with a separately pretrained, greedy wav2vec2-base-960h CTC recognizer. The latter supplied boundaries for earlier cropped panels; it does not choose utterances, frames or transcripts here. For a replication of the allocation gain, require both speaker-paired 95% intervals for allocated-minus-equal WER to be below zero. For practical equivalence to original recognition at 9 kbit/s, require both paired intervals to lie inside ±3 absolute WER points. Neither criterion measures listener preference or cross-modal generation. This frozen-codec check runs on Fedora and does not change the factorial models.
