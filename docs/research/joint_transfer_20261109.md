# G3 joint transfer study, 9 November 2026

G2 placed an entire received window behind one recurrent 512-value state. Both processor families lost motion information and caption retrieval was near chance. We test two explanations separately where possible. The retrospective probe predicts a previously observed quantity from frozen G2 states using training groups and scores old selection groups. It compares all 512 directions against the first 128, along with random cores. This localizes linear accessibility and cannot identify a unique training cause.

G3 starts from both matched 500,006,912-token text model families, seeds 81 and 82. Each frozen 12-layer parent processes the same ordered field for all modalities. Learned signal edges, shared residual adapters at every layer, decoding heads and the alignment projection update jointly. Language replay has genuine next-token targets. Direct current and one-step signal losses and independent, contrastive caption encoding supervise that same field. Text targets never enter signal observations. A camera frame is represented by a low-resolution thumbnail and static images retain spatial patches. In the robot stream only prior issued commands are observed. A future here is one ordered position; its physical duration varies by source. No policy is fitted.

## Comparisons

Five profiles per parent seed give ten fixed runs. Dense GPT-2 and dense SLWM each have adapter rank 192. Sparse SLWM executes one of four content-routed rank-192 adapters at every position. Dense rank-768 variants of both families match the sparse adapter bank's weight count. GPT-2 always uses dense updates. All variants share input order, source mix, preprocessing, optimizer and task heads within each seed. Report total and trainable parameters, nonexpert plus one expert per layer, routing loads, timings and held-out outcomes. Routing-balance regularization follows the idea in [Switch Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.03961). Sparse execution applies to the adapters; the pretrained backbone is dense. Active weight counts describe conditional capacity, not FLOPs.

Compare fixed update budgets of 100, 1,000 and 8,000 with 64 examples each. Per 100 updates the mix is 20 general text, 25 COCO, 10 unlabelled CIFAR-100, 15 LibriSpeech, 5 AMI, 5 Clotho, 10 PAMAP2 and 10 DROID. Each source traverses a seeded permutation of eligible records before reuse; windows, elapsed signal time and unique identities have different denominators. Full budget gives 512,000 presentations including 9,830,400 replay token targets, 128,000 COCO and 51,200 CIFAR images per run. The language parent has seen vastly more data than the paired update stream. Report actual unique record counts. A fourfold adapter capacity change at fixed backbone depth tests adaptation scale only.

Use only documented train roles in joint fitting. Use CIFAR's 50,000 official train images with both label bytes discarded. FSDD, Fashion-MNIST and Banking77 are excluded entirely from joint fitting. Public web-text pretraining makes incidental text overlap possible. These benchmarks are fresh downstream tasks for this project.

## Evaluation and stopping

A deterministic class-balanced ridge readout uses 1, 8 or 32 labelled examples per class, normalization from those examples only, fixed ridge penalty, and no backbone updates. The primary readout concatenates four ordered means of shared positions. Last-state pooling is the compression control. Raw features and step-zero parent+random edges are controls. FSDD uses three fit speakers, one development speaker and two final speakers as specified in config. For Fashion and Banking, a disjoint subset of the official train partition provides development and the official test partition provides confirmation. Remove exact Banking train/test text duplicates for the final comparison. Report balanced accuracy, per-speaker digit outcomes and paired uncertainty. Two final speakers restrict population inference. Compare the same examples at every capacity.

All profiles, readout recipes and budgets are fixed before running confirmation. Screen and 1,000-step development scores may be reported while larger fits continue. Open final scores only after locking all ten endpoint hashes and the evaluation code. The primary claim compares the 32-shot ordered-state scores to both the matched dense GPT-2 and the stronger raw/parent controls. Require improvement on at least two of three tasks in both seeds, without a larger than two-point regression on the third. Report sparse gains against both compute-path and total-bank dense budgets. Monitor text retention with fixed validation windows against the respective parent, and paired retrieval against permutation. These are discriminative transfer and short-horizon tests. Physical decisions require separate action-conditioned and closed-loop evidence.
