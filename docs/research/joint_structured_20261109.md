# G3 structured sparsity in the shared backbone

This companion experiment was fixed after the 100-update dense and routed-adapter screen and before the three-task confirmation. It isolates sparsity in the shared backbone, independently of sparsity in the adapters. For both signal parents, keep the two largest-magnitude weights in each group of four consecutive input coordinates in all 24 feed-forward matrices. Ties follow original index order. Preserve embeddings, attention, biases, causal signal operators and dense rank-192 adapters. The two GPT-2 controls continue to use fully dense weights.

Fit the same edges and residual adapters at 100, 1,000 and 8,000 updates. The 12-layer dense signal and dense GPT-2 results provide paired controls at each exposure. Each training trace must match its original seed's content hash. Confirm three-task scores only after the structured endpoints and matching full-depth endpoints are locked. The underlying language training is inherited. Recovery from weight pruning is part of the transfer cost.

All 24 backbone matrices remain fixed during joint adaptation, so their structural zeros persist. Training uses ordinary dense kernels. A separate 3090 inference microbenchmark compares a truly packed semi-structured weight against the identical zero-masked dense weight and checks numerical agreement. A matrix timing cannot establish whole-model acceleration. The stored parameter count, pruned entries and active adapter count have separate meanings. See the [PyTorch semi-structured sparsity documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/sparse.html).
