# G3 paired physical transfer extension

Before scoring Push-T, freeze the source archive, split salt, all ten checkpoints for each budget, readout, ablations and scoring code. This extension was designed after the first three-task G3 development scores and before any Push-T outcomes. The joint models never train on Push-T. The ten 100-step endpoints use 64 held-out demonstration episodes. The ten 8,000-step endpoints will use a different set of 94. Thirty-two fit episodes provide supervised readouts at both budgets. Sixteen further episodes are reserved. Every role consists of full episodes selected by a fixed hash of episode ID.

Each window supplies 16 observed RGB frames, pusher coordinates and previously issued commands. Each representation sees the corresponding G3 visual and numeric edges. A fixed ridge readout also receives eight recorded intended commands expressed relative to the last observed pusher position. Its target is the simulated block's displacement over those eight steps. No future image, pusher position or object position enters the readout. The historical block location is available only through the image. Commands enter the probe as a separate known input. These recorded trajectories cannot establish counterfactual action effects.

Compare identical readouts from joint, image-only and numeric-only observations, plus the joint state without a supplied plan and a held-out cross-episode plan permutation. Raw history (the same visual resolution and physical channels), zero displacement and each dense matched GPT-2 are controls. The score averages Euclidean endpoint displacement errors within episode, then across episodes, in the native 512-unit arena. Paired uncertainty resamples full episodes. Require at least 5% lower error and a positive paired lower bound relative to raw, zero, both unimodal controls and the applicable dense GPT-2 references in both seeds before claiming joint benefit. Sparse SLWM also compares with the wide dense GPT-2. Report every comparison. This tests transfer of a representation to a recorded prediction task. A decision policy would require an action objective and interventions in an environment.

The replay is the public simulated Push-T dataset from [Chi et al.](https://diffusion-policy.cs.columbia.edu/). Images and labels stay in the research artifact store.
